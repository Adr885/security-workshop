Clinic Services and Appointments

Community Health Connect is a non-profit network of three community clinics. On this page you can see which services are available today, check our opening hours and send an appointment request. A member of our team will call you to confirm the time within one working day.

In an emergency, do not use this form. Call 1122 or go to the nearest hospital.

Our Services

Every service is free for registered patients. Some services also accept walk-ins.

Walk-in

General Check-up

Consultation with a general physician for fever, cough, pain and other common problems.

Average wait: 20 minutes

Appointment

Mother and Child Care

Pregnancy check-ups, newborn care and growth monitoring for children under five.

Female doctor available

Walk-in

Vaccination

Routine childhood vaccines and seasonal flu shots. Bring the vaccination card.

No fee

Appointment

Diabetes and Blood Pressure

Regular monitoring, medicine review and diet advice for long-term conditions.

Monthly follow-up

Appointment

Dental Care

Check-ups, cleaning and simple fillings by a visiting dentist.

Tuesdays and Thursdays

Walk-in

Basic Lab Tests

Blood sugar, haemoglobin and urine tests with results on the same day.

How to Book an Appointment

  1. Choose the service you need from the list above.
  2. Fill in the appointment request form below.
  3. Wait for our call to confirm the date and time.
  4. Bring your CNIC or B-form and any old prescriptions.
